# Graph legend

This is a view of the EAP-IG greedy circuit, not a complete architectural diagram. Boxes are attention heads (`aL.hH`), MLPs (`mL`), the input residual stream, or `logits` (the terminal refusal-projection metric). Black edges are MLP/residual edges; magenta, green, and blue indicate Q, K, and V inputs respectively. Edge width is proportional to absolute score.
